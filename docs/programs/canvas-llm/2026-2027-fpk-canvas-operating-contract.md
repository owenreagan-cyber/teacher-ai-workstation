# 2026–2027 FPK Canvas Operating Contract

Status: owner-approved canonical operating contract

This document replaces earlier assumptions about how the Teacher AI Workstation should prepare Canvas content for the 2026–2027 school year.

The Canvas workflow is intentionally narrow:

1. Maintain simplified subject agenda pages.
2. Create only required graded assignments.
3. Create minimal assessment announcements.
4. Maintain a monthly Homeroom newsletter.
5. Require teacher review before any future publication action.
6. Do not use Canvas as a parent-facing curriculum-resource library.

## 1. Canvas Surfaces

### Weekly subject agenda pages

Maintain separate agenda pages for:

- Math
- Reading/Spelling
- Language Arts
- History when active
- Science when active

### Monthly page

Maintain a separate Homeroom newsletter updated once per month.

## 2. Active History and Science Quarters

- Quarter 1: History active; Science inactive.
- Quarter 2: Science active; History inactive.
- Quarter 3: History active; Science inactive.
- Quarter 4: Science active; History inactive.

The inactive subject page must remain untouched.

The system must not:

- regenerate it
- rewrite it
- publish it
- unpublish it
- change its front-page state
- create assignments for it
- create announcements for it
- create reminders for it

## 3. Weekly Agenda Page Contract

The subject agenda page may contain only:

1. Quarter/week heading, such as `Q1W1`
2. Preloaded date range
3. Reminders
4. Monday through Friday
5. `In Class`
6. `Homework`

### Reminders

Reminders may list upcoming assessment names and dates only.

Reminders must not contain:

- links
- attachments
- study-guide references
- resource references
- detailed preparation directions
- book or workbook locations

### Resource section

The resource section has been removed.

Subject agenda pages must not contain links to:

- books
- workbooks
- worksheets
- glossaries
- files
- study guides
- curriculum resources
- external resources

### Daily lesson formatting

Math:

`In Class: Lesson 18`

Reading:

`In Class: Lesson 42`

Spelling:

`In Class: Lesson 56`

Shurley English:

`In Class: Chapter 5, Lesson 3`

History:

`In Class: Ancient Rome, Lesson 4`

Science:

`In Class: Life Cycles, Lesson 2`

History and Science must use the unit title and lesson number.

### Homework formatting

Homework must follow the approved subject schedule.

When there is no homework, write:

`Homework: No Homework`

No homework entry may contain a curriculum-resource link.

## 4. Math Schedule

Course: Saxon Math 4 — Course 1

### Daily classwork goal

Monday through Friday:

`#1–10`

### Homework schedule

- Monday: `#12–30 evens`
- Tuesday: No Homework
- Wednesday: `#11–29 odds`
- Thursday: No Homework
- Friday: No Homework

### Weekly Math accuracy grades

Create three Math accuracy grades per week:

1. Monday homework
2. Wednesday homework
3. Tuesday classwork by default

If Tuesday contains a Math assessment, default the classwork grade to Thursday.

The teacher may override Tuesday or Thursday.

Do not grade:

- fact classwork
- fact homework

Assessments are not included in the three weekly accuracy grades.

## 5. Reading Schedule

Course: Reading Mastery 4–5

### Daily classwork goal

Monday through Friday:

`Workbook`

### Homework schedule

- Monday: No Homework
- Tuesday: Comprehension Questions
- Wednesday: No Homework
- Thursday: Comprehension Questions
- Friday: No Homework

### Weekly Reading accuracy grades

Create three Reading accuracy grades per week:

1. Tuesday comprehension homework
2. Thursday comprehension homework
3. Monday workbook classwork by default

The teacher may override the classwork grade to Wednesday.

Assessments are not included in the three weekly accuracy grades.

## 6. Assessment Schedule

- Math Written Assessments and Fact Assessments: Tuesday
- Reading Mastery Tests and Fluency Checkouts: Wednesday
- Shurley English assessments: Friday
- History assessments: Friday during Q1 and Q3
- Science assessments: Friday during Q2 and Q4
- Spelling tests: Friday by default

The teacher may override a Spelling test to Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday.

## 7. Announcement Contract

Assessment announcements are prepared for the Friday 4:00 PM update.

The teacher enters assessment coverage.

Announcements may contain only:

- test number, name, or topic
- what it covers
- test date
- approved general practice language when applicable

Announcements must not contain:

- study guides
- study-guide links
- attachments
- book-page references
- workbook-page references
- exact Reading checkout locations
- exact Spelling words
- curriculum-resource links

### Reading fluency language

Use general guidance such as:

> Have your child continue to practice fluency by reading a short paragraph of about 100 words aloud in less than one minute. They should make no more than 2 errors.

Do not identify the checkout passage or its page.

### Spelling practice language

Spelling tests occur every fifth lesson.

For a test on lesson `N`, the practice range is:

`N - 4` through `N - 1`

Examples:

- Test on Lesson 5 → practice Lessons 1–4
- Test on Lesson 20 → practice Lessons 16–19
- Test on Lesson 60 → practice Lessons 56–59

Do not list the exact Spelling words.

## 8. Study Guides

Study guides are not part of the 2026–2027 Canvas workflow.

The system must not generate:

- study-guide assignments
- study-guide files
- study-guide links
- study-guide attachments
- study-guide reminders
- study-guide announcements
- study-guide deployment items

Math assessment output contains only:

- Written Assessment
- Fact Assessment

## 9. Assignment Naming Contract

Every assignment title must begin with the correct course code.

Approved course-code prefixes:

- `RM4:`
- `SM5:`
- `ELA4:`
- `HIST4:`
- `SCI4:`

Examples:

- `RM4: Spelling Test 1`
- `RM4: Mastery Test 4`
- `RM4: Fluency Checkout 4`
- `SM5: Lesson 18 Homework`
- `SM5: Written Assessment 7`
- `SM5: Fact Assessment 7`
- `ELA4: Persuasive Writing Final Draft`
- `HIST4: Ancient Rome Assessment`
- `SCI4: Life Cycles Assessment`

An assignment missing the approved course-code prefix must fail validation before publication.

## 10. Assignment Due-Date and Point Rules

All assignments:

- are worth 100 points
- display as percentages
- are due on the same calendar day as the assignment
- are due at 11:59 PM

Examples:

- Monday Math homework → Monday at 11:59 PM
- Tuesday Reading homework → Tuesday at 11:59 PM
- Wednesday Math homework → Wednesday at 11:59 PM
- Thursday Reading homework → Thursday at 11:59 PM
- Friday Spelling test → Friday at 11:59 PM

## 11. Grading Weights

### Reading, Grades 2–5

- AS: 40%
- CO: 40%
- HW: 20%

AS includes:

- Reading Mastery Tests
- Spelling Tests

CO includes:

- Fluency Checkouts

HW includes:

- three Reading accuracy grades per week

Reading Mastery Tests have no retests. Recommended remediation may be provided.

Fluency Checkouts use:

- 100%
- 70%
- 50%

No third retest is given.

### Math, Course 1

- WAS: 60%
- FAS: 20%
- HW: 20%

WAS includes Written Assessments.

Written Assessment retests are for mastery purposes only.

Apply the approved label deduction:

- minus 1 point per missing or incorrect label

FAS includes Fact Assessments.

Fact Assessments have no retests.

HW includes three Math accuracy grades per week.

Do not grade fact classwork or fact homework.

### Language Arts

- AS: 70%
- CW/HW: 30%

AS includes:

- Shurley English assessments
- Spelling is not graded here
- final Writing drafts

Final Writing drafts use titles such as:

`ELA4: Persuasive Writing Final Draft`

CW/HW includes:

- Shurley accuracy work
- Writing pre-work
- bubble maps
- rough drafts
- other pre-writing work

Create one or two Language Arts accuracy grades per week.

### History and Science

History category:

- HIS: 100%

Science category:

- SCI: 100%

During each active quarter, the active subject requires:

- 8 accuracy grades
- 2 assessment grades
- no retests

The inactive subject receives no generated grades.

## 12. Homeroom Newsletter

The Homeroom newsletter is updated once per month.

It retains the established sections:

- Important Dates
- Homeroom News
- School News
- Links to school information, events, and related school resources

Homeroom links are permitted.

This is an explicit exception to the no-link rule for subject agenda pages and assignments.

## 13. Parent-Facing Link Restrictions

Do not place curriculum-resource links in:

- subject agenda pages
- assignments
- assessment announcements
- reminders

Do not link parents to:

- books
- workbooks
- worksheets
- glossaries
- answer keys
- study guides
- curriculum files

The Homeroom newsletter may contain school-information and school-event links.

## 14. Publication Safety

Nothing in this contract authorizes automatic Canvas publication.

Future publication must remain:

- teacher-reviewed
- explicitly approved
- previewed before execution
- limited to the intended course
- limited to the intended week
- auditable
- reversible when Canvas permits

The inactive History or Science course must never be modified automatically.

## 15. De-Scoped Features

The following are removed from the active Canvas LLM roadmap:

- Canvas resource grabber
- smart resource indexing
- file downloads
- file caching
- Canvas file management
- folder traversal
- module-to-file linking
- attachment automation
- curriculum-resource linking
- parent-facing resource links
- duplicate-file detection
- embeddings
- semantic resource search
- study-guide generation

Existing historical code may remain dormant until a later cleanup decision.

The parked C0G local resource-index implementation is not part of the active roadmap.

## 16. Active Product Goal

The Teacher AI Workstation should:

1. accept weekly pacing
2. generate the active subject agenda pages
3. create only required graded assignments
4. use the correct assignment groups
5. apply 100 points and percentage display
6. apply same-day 11:59 PM due dates
7. prepare minimal Friday 4:00 PM assessment announcements
8. prepare the monthly Homeroom newsletter when due
9. require teacher review
10. verify publication and student visibility

The system should create the right few Canvas items, in the right category, on the right day, with the correct short wording.
