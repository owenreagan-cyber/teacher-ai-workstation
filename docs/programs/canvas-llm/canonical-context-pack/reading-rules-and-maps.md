## Checkout coverage

## Reading Test 14 exception

Reading Test 14 has no Checkout.

Required behavior:

- do not generate Checkout 14;
- do not include Checkout 14 in announcements;
- do not identify the checkout passage or its page in parent-facing output.

## Reading announcement content

Assessment notices may include test number, date, teacher-entered coverage, and approved general fluency practice language.

They must not include exact checkout page or passage location, study guides, attachments, or curriculum-resource links.

For Reading Test 14, all Checkout-specific content must be omitted.
