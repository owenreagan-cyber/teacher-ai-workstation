⭐ Building Strong Habits

As we continue through Quarter 2, students are held to high expectations for their choices, effort, responsibility, and treatment of others.

We will continue focusing on following directions the first time, transitioning appropriately, staying organized, coming to class prepared, and making smart choices throughout the school day.

We will also be emphasizing good sportsmanship, respectful communication, handling wins and losses appropriately, and being a positive teammate and classmate. Our goal is for every student to take increasing ownership of their choices and help make our classroom a place where everyone can learn and be successful.

⭐ Behavior Tracker

Earlier this quarter, we added a new behavior tracker to the back of each student's stamp sheet. This system is designed to keep communication clear between school and home while helping students recognize patterns, make smart choices, and grow.

After one verbal reminder, continued behaviors such as talking out, not tapping and tracking, or distracting others may result in a warning tally.

  • Tallies reset every week, giving students a fresh start each Monday.
  • 5 tallies in one week: the student will not visit the prize box that Friday, and a Grow Note will come home.
  • More than 5 tallies: an additional consequence may be needed, such as a 5-minute recess sit-out.
  • If patterns continue into future weeks, we may reach out to schedule a parent meeting so we can work together.

More serious choices, including lying, cheating, physical contact, or disrespect, may result in a Grow Note immediately.
